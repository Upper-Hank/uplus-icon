## Before submitting

Explain meaning, use cases, relationships to existing icons, and whether a new asset is needed. Formal SVGs must be supplied or approved file-by-file by the project owner. Screenshots, annotated design exports, and auto-generated outlines never enter the raw directory.

## Change scope

- **MUST** keep metadata, generator, runtime, and site changes in reviewable responsibility boundaries.
- **MUST NOT** format, optimize, or batch-rewrite unrelated raw SVGs.
- **SHOULD** accompany API changes with docs, type checks, and consumer examples.

## Review evidence

Icon work includes the target-size matrix, light/dark backgrounds, and peer comparisons. Engineering work lists commands run, bundle impact, and compatibility. Motion work additionally covers part mapping, reduced motion, and rapid retriggering.
